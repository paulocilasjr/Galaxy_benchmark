## Summary

**Analysis pipeline on Galaxy (usegalaxy.org):**

1. **DESeq2** differential expression comparing **CBD_IC50 vs DMSO** (vehicle control) in CRC cells
2. **g:Profiler GOSt** GO Biological Process enrichment on up- and down-regulated gene sets separately (padj < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.5)

**Results:**

| Metric | Up-regulated | Down-regulated |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| DE genes | 59 | 122 |
| GO:BP terms | 781 | 959 |
| Core metabolic GO:BP terms | 66 | 172 |

**Key down-regulated metabolic terms** (highly significant):
- Carbohydrate metabolic process (p=2.86×10⁻¹¹)
- Monosaccharide metabolic process (p=1.48×10⁻⁹)
- Glucose metabolic process (p=5.05×10⁻⁷)
- Pyruvate metabolic process (p=8.56×10⁻⁷)
- Organic acid metabolic process (p=7.31×10⁻⁷)

**Key up-regulated metabolic terms** (less significant):
- Lipid metabolic process (p=2.14×10⁻⁴)
- Catabolic process (p=1.08×10⁻³)

**Answer: downregulation** — with ~2× more DE genes, ~2.6× more core metabolic GO:BP terms, and far more significant enrichment for specific energy metabolism pathways (carbohydrate, glucose, pyruvate), downregulation of gene expression primarily drives the metabolic effects of CBD treatment in CRC cells.
