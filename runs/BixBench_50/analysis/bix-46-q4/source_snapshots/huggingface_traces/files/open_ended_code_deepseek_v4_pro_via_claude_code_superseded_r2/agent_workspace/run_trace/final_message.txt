The log2 fold change for `PA14_35160` (pumA, phenazine-utilizing monooxygenase A) in the ΔrhlI mutant is **-4.10**.

Here's how I arrived at this:

- The two RDS files (`res_1vs97.rds` and `res_1vs98.rds`) are DESeq2 DESeqResults objects containing differential expression results from two comparisons.
- **`res_1vs97`** corresponds to the ΔrhlI mutant: all major phenazine biosynthesis genes (phzA1–F1, phzA2–F2, phzM, phzS, pumA) are strongly downregulated (log2FC ranging from −2.15 to −6.01) with highly significant adjusted p-values. This matches the known biology — RhlI produces the autoinducer for the Rhl quorum sensing system, which positively regulates phenazine production; its deletion abolishes this regulation.
- **`res_1vs98`** shows no consistent downregulation of phenazine genes (mixed, mostly non-significant changes), ruling it out as the ΔrhlI comparison.
- In `res_1vs97`, PA14_35160 has a log2 fold change of −4.099114, which rounds to −4.10 (2 decimal places).
