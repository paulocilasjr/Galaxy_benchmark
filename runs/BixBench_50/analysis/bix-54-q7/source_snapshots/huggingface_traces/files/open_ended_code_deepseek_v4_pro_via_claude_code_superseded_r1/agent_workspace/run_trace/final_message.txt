The analysis is complete. Here's the summary:

**Data**: The `Swarm_2.csv` dataset contains colony area measurements for pure strains (287, 98) and 287:98 mixtures at various ratios. Strain 1 was excluded as it's a different strain system, leaving 42 observations (3 replicates each at 14 proportion levels from 0 to 1).

**Models fitted**: Quadratic, cubic, and natural spline (df=4), all predicting colony Area from the proportion of strain 287.

**Best model**: Natural spline with df=4 was the clear winner:
- AIC: 980.9 (vs 1016.6 for cubic, 1034.5 for quadratic)
- Adjusted R²: 0.787 (vs 0.491 for cubic, 0.204 for quadratic)
- ANOVA: natural spline significantly improves over cubic (F=53.7, p<0.001)

**Result**: The natural spline model predicts a maximum colony area of **178983.9** at a strain 287 proportion of approximately 0.898.
