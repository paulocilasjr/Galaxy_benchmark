## Summary

The analysis was completed on Galaxy using a custom UDT (`swarm-distance-v2`) that:

1. **Grouped the CSV data** by `StrainNumber` and `Ratio`, computing mean Area and Circularity for each group
2. **Computed z-score normalized Euclidean distance** between each mixed culture (Strain 287 + Strain 98) ratio and the Strain 1 reference, accounting for the different scales of Area and Circularity
3. **Identified the closest ratio**: **5:1** (Strain 287:Strain 98)

### Key results:

| Ratio | Mean Area | Mean Circularity | Z-score Distance |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Strain 1** | 111,960 | 0.0613 | — (reference) |
| **5:1** | 118,086 | 0.0667 | **0.119** |
| 3:1 | 89,125 | 0.0857 | 0.446 |
| 2:1 | 87,057 | 0.0963 | 0.494 |
| 50:1 | 93,919 | 0.0950 | 0.368 |

The **5:1 ratio** of Strain 287 to Strain 98 produces colonies whose mean area (~118K) and circularity (~0.067) are closest to Strain 1's values (~112K area, ~0.061 circularity).
