**Extended Data Fig. 4 \| Tool inventory, tool-set similarity and answer matching.**
**a**, The 15 installed Galaxy tools with completed jobs in the most Galaxy runs, ranked overall, with each model's share of its own traced Galaxy runs (the inventory shown as panel a in the first version of Fig. 4).
**b**, Mean tool-set similarity by model and benchmark, over task–model cells in which all three Galaxy runs completed a job.
*P* values test a model effect, permuting model labels within tasks (20,000 permutations).
The table gives the panel c correlation under alternative definitions: with UDT items dropped, for cells whose three runs used installed tools only, and for cells with any UDT.
**c**, Share of replicate sets with the same answer in all three runs under three answer-matching rules, for 600 sets per condition (BixBench-Verified-50 and CompBioBench).
Intervals are 95% percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals (20,000 resamples; clusters are BixBench source capsules, otherwise tasks).
