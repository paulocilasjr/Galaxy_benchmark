Here's a summary of the analysis:

**Pipeline executed on Galaxy (usegalaxy.org):**

1. **PhyKIT RCV — Animals** (`scogs_animals.zip`): Computed Relative Composition Variability on 241 single-copy ortholog alignments from 4 animal species (*C. elegans, G. gallus, M. musculus, X. laevis*).

2. **PhyKIT RCV — Fungi** (`scogs_fungi.zip`): Computed RCV on 255 ortholog alignments from 4 fungal species (*N. crassa, S. cerevisiae, S. pombe, U. maydis*).

3. **Mann-Whitney U test** (two-sided, auto method with continuity correction): Compared the two RCV score distributions.

**Result:**
- **U-statistic**: 5,483.5
- **p-value**: **1.5197572608715265 × 10⁻⁵⁶**
- Animal RCV distribution is significantly lower than fungal RCV — fungi show substantially greater relative composition variability across their single-copy orthologs.
