# Review of the two Galaxy manuscript narratives

Review date: 2 October 2026.

Both packages are substantial, coherent baseline drafts. Neither is ready for a Nature Methods submission. Their main limitations are scientific inference, evaluation validity and independence of the two contributions. The editable text, vector figures, preserved evidence and figure-generation code provide a strong foundation for revision.

The user-oriented paper should ask when an execution environment improves the reliability and reviewability of biomedical agent analyses, and at what cost. The Galaxy-oriented paper should identify and validate the execution contracts scientific workbenches need to serve agents. Treat Galaxy superiority as a hypothesis, and let the results determine the recommendation.

## Review coverage

The review inventoried the project, read its governing documents and principal analysis/manuscript materials, audited the complete 4,240-run aggregate and the 160 task-evidence packages, and followed pivotal cases into their trace-indexed evidence. Both Markdown manuscripts, DOCX contents, figure scripts and Source Data workbooks were reviewed. Both DOCX files were rendered to 20-page reading copies; all twelve main figures were visually inspected, including their PDF renders. Binary datasets and the hundreds of thousands of raw archive files were inventoried and inspected selectively rather than read byte by byte. Hidden ground-truth files and credentials were not opened. No scientific task was rerun and no Galaxy server was contacted.

The original manuscripts and figures are unchanged. This document is an assessment and revision plan.

## Overall assessment

The archive is a scientific asset. Independent checks found unique run keys and matching source hashes for all 160 task evidence files. These checks establish preservation and traceability, not independently verified biological correctness or successful re-execution.

## What Nature Methods exemplars suggest

The following are examples to study, not templates to imitate word for word. The editorial lessons are this review's interpretation of the accessible papers and journal guidance. CellVoyager and the 2026 foundation-model Perspective have subscription-limited main text; their accessible abstracts, figure listings and availability information were used.

| Publication | What it contributes to this revision |
|---|---|
| Luecken et al., [Benchmarking atlas-level data integration in single-cell genomics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01336-8), Nature Methods 19, 41–50 (2022) | Organizes heterogeneous tasks and metrics around biological preservation, usability and scalability; ends with method-selection guidance and releases a reusable evaluation pipeline. The Galaxy comparison should similarly deliver conditional decisions and a reusable measurement system. |
| Alber et al., [CellVoyager](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6), Nature Methods 23, 749–759 (2026) | Connects an agent method to a benchmark, expert evaluations and three biological case studies. Its figure sequence moves from system and performance to human assessment and biological results. Galaxy's user paper needs independently measured utility and biological consequences. |
| Ahlmann-Eltze et al., [Deep-learning-based gene perturbation effect prediction does not yet outperform simple linear baselines](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6), Nature Methods 22, 1657–1661 (2025) | Shows that critical comparisons with strong simple baselines can be influential. The relevant lesson is to earn conclusions through controls and metric scrutiny, including a pinned, reproducible code baseline. |
| Heil et al., [Reproducibility standards for machine learning in the life sciences](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01256-7), Nature Methods 18, 1132–1135 (2021) | Makes third-party reproduction operational through available data, dependencies, code and automation. Retained histories should be connected to successful independent replay. |
| Saez-Rodriguez et al., [Benchmarking biomedical foundation models](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03182-y), Nature Methods 23, 1724–1733 (2026) | Positions transparent evaluation, reproducibility and generalization as central concerns. This is current background for a benchmark whose final-answer scores can conceal scientific or infrastructure failures. |

The 2025 perturbation benchmark should also be read alongside the new [calibrated-metric study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w), published in Nature Biotechnology on 1 October 2026. Together they make metric validity a timely issue; they are not evidence about Galaxy performance.

Update the novelty discussion to engage recent agent work. [Paper2Agent](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11044-y) already studies executable scientific agents and tool verification. Biomni now has a published [Science article](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz4351), whereas the local references still list a preprint with an unresolved DOI. Neither manuscript should imply that agent orchestration of scientific tools is itself new. The potential advance here is controlled evaluation of the execution environment, or a validated portable execution contract.

## Priority 0 Corrections that affect conclusions

### 1 Correct or relabel the majority-vote analysis

[fig_on_demand.py:436](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/manuscript_material/scripts/fig_on_demand.py:436) groups every evaluator-correct answer into an artificial `CORRECT` label before counting votes. This computes whether at least two runs were graded correct. It does not demonstrate that a user could select a correct answer by comparing the submitted answers without reference access.

An independent diagnostic using the documented ten-significant-digit answer grouping found eight paired BixBench replicate sets that the oracle classified as correct but that have no answer majority. This is a check of the published aggregation rule, not a replacement scientific grading policy. A prespecified semantic or tolerance-aware voting rule could resolve some disagreements differently.

| Configuration | Published pooled code / Galaxy result | Answer vote under the documented rule |
|---|---:|---:|
| GPT-5.5 | 88.0% / 88.0% | 88.0% / 88.0% |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | 91.33% / 92.0% | 90.0% / 91.33% |
| GPT-5.6 Luna | 88.0% / 84.67% | 88.0% / 84.0% |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro | 86.0% / 83.33% | 84.0% / 82.67% |

The claim that three code runs exceed one Galaxy run for every configuration therefore fails under the documented rule: Sol code voting gives 90.0%, below its pooled single-run Galaxy value of 90.89%. The pooled table is shown only to diagnose the existing panel; the revised paper should report benchmark-specific endpoints.

Required change: aggregate answers using an outcome-blind, declared rule for numbers, units, textual answers and no-consensus cases, then score the selected answer. Alternatively label the existing statistic “at least two of three runs scored correct” and remove its interpretation as a deployable user strategy. Revise user Fig. 6d, its text and the dependent cost recommendation.

### 2 Distinguish reconstructed CompBio grades from independently verified correctness

[individual_error_analysis.md:86](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/individual_error_analysis.md:86) distinguishes 54 score-inferred items from 46 score-predicted items that are not uniquely determined. Reproducing all aggregate leaderboard totals does not uniquely validate every item grade. These grades affect repeatability, unanimous accuracy, voting, recovery and tool-trajectory comparisons.

The project also contains incompatible evidence versions. Canonical CompBio audits retain mixed official/predicted aggregate records and unresolved submission issues. A later private-lab snapshot is reported to resolve the hashes and provide all 25 official totals. The narrative uses this later reconstructed key without fully reconciling the older canonical layers. “87.1% accuracy” needs its reconstructed-reference qualification wherever it is used.

Required change: freeze one authoritative data release; publish submission receipts, versioned answer vectors, normalization rules and key provenance; obtain official item evaluations or independent scientific adjudication. Until then, report reconstructed-reference agreement and sensitivity or bounds across compatible keys. Keep aggregate official scores distinct from reconstructed per-item outcomes.

The Methods also misdescribe grading: [fig_on_demand.py:406](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/manuscript_material/scripts/fig_on_demand.py:406) uses stripped exact strings plus an enhancer-label substitution. Case/space and ten-significant-digit normalization belong to answer grouping, not that grade function.

### 3 Remove circular or biologically incomplete validation

The IWC ATAC audit records route references calibrated from the same agent runs being scored, which then score 1.0 against themselves. See [individual_error_analysis.md:2555](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/individual_error_analysis.md:2555). Those scores are useful archive descriptors, not independent validation.

The RNA-seq composite excludes significant-set agreement. Scores near 1 coexist with 12 versus 13 significant genes, and a Galaxy edgeR analysis calls 37 genes against a DESeq2 reference's 13. This does not automatically make the allowed edgeR method invalid; it demonstrates that the aggregate metric misses a consequential change in interpretation.

Required change: independently prepare route-appropriate references and separate calibration from evaluation. Report scientifically meaningful components such as significant-gene overlap, effect direction and magnitude, interval precision/recall, organism identification and prompt-contract violations. Keep scientific validity, prompt compliance, reference agreement and execution integrity separate, as the project guide intends.

### 5 Replace successful status with measured semantic fidelity

The Galaxy readiness score calls `status == "ok"` “ran exactly as requested” ([script:807](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/manuscript_narrative/galaxy-oriented/scripts/make_figures.py:807)). The known conditional-branch loophole returned ok with `checked_parameter_count: 0` ([report:191](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/Galaxy_improvement_report.md:191)). A successful call with no detected mismatch is not proof of faithful execution.

Required change: rename the existing endpoint and separately measure check coverage, requested/resolved/executed parameter agreement, dataset binding, tool/container version, output semantics and unverified cases. Make the scientific intent-to-execution relationship the Galaxy paper's central object.

## Priority 1 Requirements for defensible scientific claims

### Comparison and uncertainty

The four-Codex BixBench comparison is 519/600 versus 511/600 accepted answers: +1.33 percentage points with a 95% interval of approximately −3.48 to +6.34. It establishes similar observed acceptance, with substantial uncertainty. It does not establish superiority, equivalence or non-inferiority. Most platform-neutral repeatability intervals also include no improvement.

Replace the user title's “without loss of accuracy,” “preserves accuracy” and causal “pinned tools remove” language. Use “similar observed benchmark accuracy,” “fewer discordant scored outcomes,” and “divergence associated with implementation/version differences was less frequent.” A future non-inferiority claim needs a scientifically justified margin and a powered prospective design.

The paired design does not isolate the environment. Shared labels do not guarantee identical runtime model snapshots, reasoning settings, harnesses, prompts or selection policies. CompBio final vectors can contain continuations and recovery campaigns. Raw IWC metadata show 6- and 12-hour budgets, with only 73/120 same-label replicate pairs budget-matched. Publish these differences rather than burying them in limitations.

Required prospective controls: common scientific task wording and information access; fixed model/harness versions; equal time and resource budgets; isolated accounts; independent repetitions; declared retry/selection policy; and complete costs across campaigns and subagents. Include a locked/containerized code baseline with provenance, alongside flexible code and Galaxy, to separate version standardization from other Galaxy benefits.

Preserve source-capsule clustering for BixBench and task clustering elsewhere. Calls, jobs, runs, task/configuration cells and task cases are different units. Report per-panel eligible samples and uncertainty at the level relevant to the inference. Use benchmark-specific endpoints; avoid pooling BixBench and CompBio immediately after saying benchmarks are never pooled. With only ten IWC tasks, show task-level results and leave-one-task-out sensitivity prominently.

### Reproducibility and user benefit

Repeated agent attempts measure outcome repeatability. Replaying a fixed analysis on a clean environment measures computational reproducibility. Preserved histories provide provenance. These must be named and measured separately.

Detailed histories are present for 714/750 BixBench, 1,198/1,200 CompBio and 118/120 IWC Galaxy runs, totaling 2,030/2,070. Forty lack detailed history evidence. Both conditions have retained traces; “every failure stayed traceable” and “every wrong answer attributable” are too strong.

For the user paper, add independent reconstruction/replay with frozen inputs, tools, containers and tolerances. Measure replay success, time to reconstruct, human interventions, provenance completeness, analytical-error detection and scientific acceptability. Have domain experts adjudicate multiple acceptable solutions; blind output assessment where practical, while acknowledging that execution traces reveal condition. Include duplicate review, agreement statistics, disagreement resolution and adjudication confidence.

The current UDT audit used AI subagents, one reviewer per run, and a codebook amendment after the first pass. That is transparently documented in [the audit README](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/manuscript_material/on_demand/udt_audit/README.md), but is not independent human validation. Describe the AI assistance and add independently reviewed evidence.

### Integrity and execution location

The counts of eighteen local-answer computations, two cross-run copies and twenty-six answer-retrieval task cases come from a review concentrated on problematic cases. They are observed findings, not exhaustive prevalence estimates across all runs. The IWC pseudobulk case additionally records local preprocessing and a notebook route; eighteen local answers is not a total count of all computation outside Galaxy.

Audit all runs or an outcome-independent sample. Report the assigned experimental arm, where evidence-bearing computation actually occurred, and whether the answer was exposed to benchmark material. Provide contamination/compliance sensitivity analyses without claiming that post hoc exclusions remove selection bias.

Raw API calls still create Galaxy job provenance. Distinguish shell-side Galaxy API access, bypassing the adapter's parameter guard, local scientific computation, and cross-run reuse. A shell command used for downloading an output is different from computing the answer locally.

### Mechanistic examples

- **bix-30-q3:** differences involve raw versus normalized data, sample exclusions, test choice and information access. Three Galaxy runs accessed the ideal answer. The audit explicitly says the score gap does not measure execution-platform quality ([audit:503](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/individual_error_analysis.md:503)). Recast this as a task/reference ambiguity and contamination example.
- **bix-45-q1:** thirteen Galaxy runs used the wrapper and two used UDTs ([audit:420](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/individual_error_analysis.md:420)). Current-definition outputs are judged scientifically defensible by the audit. Distinguish version visibility from scientific error and benchmark rejection.
- **bix-12-q5:** the two code failures are from the superseded Claude Code harness; the primary four Codex configurations succeed in both conditions. Label the harness-specific result or replace it as the primary mechanism illustration.
- **PepQuery:** wrapper version and score are associated. “Whichever wrapper was found first decided the score” needs controlled search-order or metadata intervention, or weaker observational wording.

### Recovery and resources

The current recovery measure means an error occurred in a run that ultimately scored correct. It does not show that the failed step recovered, the same scientific goal was retained, or the fix caused the final success. Use linked failure→repair→successful execution→scientific output records and distinguish operational from scientific recovery.

Token analyses measure context consumption, not complete price or scientific compute. Cached tokens, uncached tokens, output/reasoning tokens, all campaigns/subagents, wall time, queue time, CPU/GPU use and storage should be reported separately when available. No current measurement supports “context, not computation, dominates cost.” Inspecting tools that are later unused can be rational selection rather than waste.

The narrative token helper accepts cells with missing usage because medians ignore missing values ([user script:74](/Users/4475918/Projects/Galaxy_benchmark/manuscript_narrative/user-oriented/scripts/make_figures.py:74)). Fourteen CompBio cells have incomplete triplicate usage, whereas the canonical analysis requires all six condition totals. Freeze eligibility, show complete-cell estimates and missingness sensitivity, and include campaign costs before making user cost recommendations.

## Distinct narrative and figure plans

### User-oriented paper

Provisional title: **Execution environments shape the reliability of biomedical AI agents**.

Central question: under which task, model and budget conditions does Galaxy increase scientifically acceptable and independently reproducible analyses per unit of user effort?

Results sequence: comparison design and task validity → benchmark outcomes with uncertainty → outcome repeatability and stable errors → implementation/version mechanisms → independent replay and user audit → resource trade-offs and conditional recommendations.

| 2 Accuracy and scientific acceptability | Keep benchmark-specific paired differences and intervals. Separate original evaluator acceptance, reconstructed-reference agreement and expert scientific adjudication. Include IWC component/sensitivity results. |

The field-moving result would be a demonstrated gain in independently reproducible, scientifically acceptable analyses or in review effort under a fair comparison. The retrospective archive can motivate and inform that validation; its current point estimates alone do not deliver it.

Provisional retrospective title: **Measuring agent readiness in scientific workbenches through Galaxy**.

A validated intervention paper could use **Execution contracts for reliable agent-operated scientific workbenches**.

Central question: what information and guarantees must a workbench expose so that an agent's intended analysis is faithfully executed, diagnostically recoverable and attributable?

Results sequence: historical deployment and interface layers → interaction failures → semantic fidelity → recovery → context efficiency → execution attribution → tested, portable requirements.

Build targeted interventions from the evidence: strict typed binding with parameter read-back; explicit versions/output semantics; structured diagnostic receipts; compact ranked discovery; stable dataset identity; complete lifecycle operations; isolated runs; execution-location attestation. Use held-out tasks and multiple model configurations. A second Galaxy deployment tests deployment robustness; implementing the same contract on another workbench tests cross-platform transfer. Listing Nextflow, Snakemake, KNIME and Jupyter establishes relevance, not transfer validation.

The failure rates describe a historical Galaxy+adapter+harness combination. Linked histories span July to early September 2026 and source collection occurred 20–23 September. Multiple BixBench image variants and uneven metadata coverage prevent interpretation as a universal or current Galaxy-core audit. Publish server versions, interface commits, wrapper/container/catalog/reference snapshots, dates and outages; explicitly retain unknowns.

## Independence of the two manuscripts

The papers currently reuse accuracy, tokens, replicate divergence, parameter mismatch and integrity findings. Distinct audiences alone do not establish distinct scientific contributions.

Assign the user paper the environment comparison, independent reproduction/user-effort measurements and decision trade-offs. Assign the Galaxy paper semantic contracts, diagnosis/context interventions, execution attestation and transfer validation. Share only necessary design and archive context; give each independent primary endpoints, new validation and its own main figures. Disclose and cite shared data and companion manuscripts to editors. If both remain retrospective narratives of essentially the same results, a single integrated Analysis may be scientifically stronger.

## Writing and presentation adjustments

1. Open with the unresolved scientific problem, the precise intervention/comparison and the contribution. Reduce generic descriptions of AI agents and established Galaxy advantages.
2. Define the experimental condition as an assigned arm. State that the arm does not certify that every scientific operation occurred in Galaxy.
3. Use claim-sized Results headings. Put the effect, unit of analysis, uncertainty, mechanism and scope together in each paragraph.
4. Replace long assertive panel titles with short measurement labels. Several titles contain complete arguments that belong in Results or legends. Galaxy Fig. 1d's title extends into the adjacent Fig. 1c region and needs a layout fix.
5. Preserve the consistent palette, marker shapes, vector text and comparative uncertainty. Avoid making filled symbols or color alone imply established benefit.
6. Report biological consequences alongside scores: significant-gene membership, peak-set changes, contaminant identity, metric definitions and version-dependent results. Explain whether the consequence is scientific, contractual or evaluative.
7. Remove universal formulations such as “every failure,” “same accuracy,” “whole workbench,” and “measures that transfer” unless their scope and validation support them.
8. Complete the human-review method, authorship, funding, competing interests, benchmark citations and stable archive/code links. Remove baseline-production notes and proposed supplementary inventories from submission text.

## Journal and package requirements

According to the current [Nature Methods content guidance](https://www.nature.com/nmeth/content), Analysis is the natural format for comprehensive comparisons of existing methods; Article is for a novel method/tool with strong validation. Resource could be appropriate if the released benchmark/trace dataset is itself the principal broadly useful contribution.

For Article, Analysis and Resource, the guidance specifies an unreferenced abstract up to 150 words, main text normally 3,000 words, and up to six figures and/or tables; longer text is possible at editorial discretion. Introduction is unheaded, Results/Methods use topical subheadings, and Discussion has a section heading but no subheadings. These are journal requirements; the experiments recommended above are this review's scientific recommendations.

## Recommended order of work

1. Freeze evidence versions; resolve reconstructed/circular grading and eligibility; correct voting, counts, semantic-fidelity labeling and factual cases. Regenerate dependent tables and figures.
2. Establish distinct primary questions and publication format. Rewrite titles, abstracts and Results claims to match the corrected retrospective evidence.
3. Run a focused, prospective validation with independent references, isolation, matched budgets, reproducible code controls and independent replay/human review. Size it for the primary scientific effect rather than the count of calls.
4. For the Galaxy paper, implement and validate the interface contract on held-out tasks; add deployment or platform transfer evidence in proportion to the general claim.
5. Rebuild paper-specific figures, Source Data, supplementary packages and release instructions; finish journal formatting and declarations.

The strongest papers will deliver different usable advances: evidence-based choices for scientists, and tested execution guarantees for platform developers.
